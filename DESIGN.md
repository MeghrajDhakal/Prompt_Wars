## Brand & Style

This design system reimagines basic mobile e-commerce storefronts into an avant-garde, luxury editorial catalog. Blending classical fashion magazine prestige with sharp, contemporary digital fluidity, the aesthetic balances tactile warmth, dramatic typography, and curated negative space.

### Key Pillars
- **Curated & Discerning:** Every product is presented as an artifact or exhibition piece rather than standard SKU inventory. 
- **Editorial High-Contrast:** The interface pairs delicate, high-contrast serif headlines with razor-sharp geometric grotesque details, framing products against warm gallery neutrals and obsidian depths.
- **Fluid & Tactile:** Micro-interactions, pill-shaped tags, dynamic glass sheets, and floating toolbars evoke a responsive, physical sensation under fingertip control.
- **Target Audience:** Design-conscious tastemakers, luxury accessories shoppers, and digital natives who appreciate high-end craft, minimalist sophistication, and friction-free tactile commerce.

## Colors

The palette transforms cold monospaced tech grays into warm, tactile museum neutrals paired with deep ink tones and vibrant accents.

- **Primary (`#121214` - Obsidian Ink):** Used for authoritative typography, primary button fills, razor borders, and luxury dark-mode accents.
- **Secondary (`#2B4CFF` - Electric Cobalt):** A vibrant punch color used sparingly for interactive highlights, active badges, live stock markers, and curated editorial calls to action.
- **Tertiary (`#D19B53` - Patina Bronze):** A metallic neutral evoking leatherware, hardware, and premium craftsmanship.
- **Neutral (`#F8F6F2` - Gallery Alabaster):** A warm off-white canvas that softens the harshness of sterile white backdrops, making leather goods and accessories stand out with organic richness.

### Application Guidelines
- Reserve `#2B4CFF` for intentional focal moments—never flood surfaces with it.
- Use `border-subtle` (`#E7E4DC`) for hairline borders (1px) around product tiles to maintain crispness without visual clutter.
- In dark surfaces or evening modes, reverse the contrast hierarchy: canvas shifts to `#121214` with `#FFFFFF` and muted ivory text.

## Typography

The typographic voice pairs **Playfair Display** (haute-couture editorial presence) with **Space Grotesk** (technical modernism for metadata/pricing) and **Geist** (clarity for descriptions and operational labels).

- **Playfair Display:** Exclusive to section mastheads, category titles, and editorial narratives. It establishes an elevated, timeless tone.
- **Space Grotesk:** Applied to prices, collection codes, micro-labels, and filtering controls. All caps tracking (`0.06em` to `0.12em`) balances the classical serif with precision styling.
- **Geist:** Ensures legibility in product attributes, specifications, basket tallies, and extended copy.

## Layout & Spacing

Mobile-first editorial layouts avoid cramped tabular e-commerce rows in favor of breathing room, deliberate rhythm, and cinematic pacing.

### Layout Mechanics
- **Device Grid:** 4-column layout on mobile (360px–480px) with `margin: 1.25rem` (20px) and `gutter: 1rem` (16px). Expands to 8 columns on tablet and 12 columns on desktop.
- **Editorial Asymmetry:** Feature key products using full-bleed hero cards (`4 col` span on mobile) interspersed with alternating 2-column discovery grids to break monotonous scrolling.
- **Touch Targets:** Minimum interactive area is 44×44px, even when housing small micro-tags or icon buttons.
- **Floating Safe Insets:** Floating bottom navigation panels maintain a `1.5rem` bottom clearance with `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through translucent optical glass, sharp tonal contrast, and diffused warm ambient shadows rather than heavy artificial drop shadows.

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Uncoated matte background (`#F8F6F2`).
- **Level 1 (Product Cards):** Pure white surface (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px border (`#E7E4DC`) and a soft, warm ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(18, 18, 20, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(18, 18, 20, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Overlays & Quick-Buy Drawers):** `#FFFFFF` with `box-shadow: 0 16px 36px -8px rgba(18, 18, 20, 0.12)`.
- **Level 3 (Floating Editorial Nav & Action Bars):** Frosted glass styling using `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`, and a subtle hairline border: `border: 1px solid rgba(18, 18, 20, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The geometric strategy juxtaposes structured rectangular imagery with smooth pill-shaped functional components.

- **Product Imagery Containers:** `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px) or `rounded` (0.5rem / 8px) with subtle internal shadows and clean perimeter clipping.
- **Pill System:** Category chips, price tags, interactive CTA buttons, and notification counters use full pill curvature (`border-radius: 9999px`) to create an organic, touch-inviting feel against crisp rectangular product photos.
- **Floating Modals & Sheets:** Top corners rounded at 24px (`rounded-xl` equivalent) to reflect gentle upward sheets.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Action (Dark Luxe):** Obsidian background (`#121214`), stark white text (`#FFFFFF`), full pill curvature (`rounded-full`), height `48px`. Contains uppercase `label-caps` typography with subtle letter-spacing.
- **Editorial Accent Action:** Electric Cobalt (`#2B4CFF`) solid button used strictly for immediate checkout or high-priority conversions.
- **Secondary Ghost:** 1px border (`#121214`), transparent background, `#121214` label. On active press, transitions smoothly to `#121214` with white text.

### 2. Product Presentation Cards
- **Editorial Aspect Ratios:** Product images use a tailored 4:5 or 3:4 aspect ratio with seamless, neutral-toned product studio shots.
- **Floating Wishlist Trigger:** Minimalist 36px circular pill button anchored top-right with frosted glass backdrop blur and a fine vector heart glyph.
- **Integrated Price Capsule:** Floating pill badge located bottom-left or bottom-right over the image container featuring a solid white or obsidian micro-capsule displaying `price-tag` typography.

### 3. Category & Filter Chips
- **Standard State:** Gallery alabaster surface (`#F8F6F2`), 1px subtle border (`#E7E4DC`), `text-muted`, full pill radius.
- **Selected State:** Solid `#121214` fill with `#FFFFFF` text, commanding immediate visual focus.
- **Counter Sub-pills:** Embedded circular badge within the chip rendered in `#2B4CFF` with white micro numerals.

### 4. Floating Island Navigation
- **Docked Floating Bar:** Positioned 16px above the device viewport base. Formed with high-blur frosted acrylic glass (`rgba(255,255,255,0.88)`), pill shaped, housing key navigation destinations (Discover, Search, Curated, Bag).
- **Cart Indicator:** Pill badge housing bag count highlighted in Electric Cobalt (`#2B4CFF`).

### 5. Input Fields & Form Controls
- **Refined Search:** Pill-shaped input with subtle inner line border (`#E7E4DC`), inset lens glyph, and placeholder styled in `body-md` muted tone. Active focus state gains a 1px solid `#121214` edge ring without harsh drop shadows.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Minimalist circular checkboxes (18px) that fill with `#121214` and check marks when selected, maintaining precise geometric alignment with accompanying filter labels.

### 6. Editorial Highlights & Badges
- **Curator’s Pick / Edition Tags:** Translucent obsidian pill (`rgba(18,18,20,0.7)`) with white `label-caps` text floating over showcase imagery.
- **Limited Release / Sale:** Deep coral red (`#FF3823`) micro-dot accompanied by clean, monospaced pricing delta markers.
